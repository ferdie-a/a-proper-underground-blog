Hello from the underground

This is the first post on a small blog that lives in a folder of text files. There is no database, no dashboard and no account to sign into, apart from the ones that already hold the code.

Each post is one file. The first line is the title, the rest is the text, and the name of the file carries the date. When I publish, the file lands in a repository, a build turns it into a page, and the page goes online a minute later. That is the whole machine.

I write most of it on the phone, usually in the gaps of a day: on a bus, in a queue, ten minutes before sleep. The editor keeps everything on the device first, so a tunnel or a dead signal does not cost a sentence. When the network comes back, the text goes up.

Nothing here is clever. The pages are plain, there is one font, and the only decoration is the space around the words. If a line is long, it wraps. If a paragraph is short, it stays short.

I wanted a place that asks nothing of me except the writing. Platforms ask for a profile picture, a newsletter, a schedule, a strategy. A folder asks for a file.

So this is the deal I made with myself: a post when there is something to say, nothing when there is not, and no apology for the gap. The archive is a list of dates and titles. You can read it from the bottom up or from the top down; it does not mind.

Welcome, and thanks for reading this far.
